What JERP does not do

Know the limits before you rely on it.

  • Complete protection from prompt injection. No detector can promise that.
  • Detection of deepfake video or audio.
  • Antivirus, file scanning, or device protection.
  • KYC, AML, or sanctions screening.
  • Legal, medical, or financial advice, or investment recommendations.
  • Refunds or insurance for fraud.
  • An explanation of why a business metric changed.
  • Review scores or paid review feeds.
  • Judgements about people: hiring, credit, insurance, access to services, or performance.
  • An uptime guarantee beyond the stated service level.

Questions about JERP

The most common questions about JERP, with a short answer to each.

What is JERP?

JERP is a fact-checking layer between your AI and its answer. Your agent — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or your own — asks JERP before it answers, and every answer comes back as true, false, or not a fact, with a confidence score and a source. It works over MCP and a REST API, and the free tier needs no account.

How does JERP reduce token costs?

Your model stops reading whole websites. JERP reads the pages on its side and hands back a short answer, so your model reads only the result. On one measured 24-site check that is about 4,700 tokens instead of 67,800 — roughly 14× fewer. The per-model cost table is in the Pricing section above.

How does JERP stop AI hallucinations?

No source, no claim. Every fact in an answer is checked against what the site itself says and against public records; anything JERP cannot source is cut instead of being guessed. When JERP is unsure, the confidence score says so rather than hiding it.

Which tools does JERP have?

Three. guard screens text, links, and actions before your AI reads or does them. verdict says whether an answer is true, false, or not a fact, with a confidence score, and what to check next. speaker gives your AI sourced facts within a size limit. The checks behind them run on our side, and every result has a receipt. Setup per host is on the integrations page.

Can JERP stop prompt injection?

It lowers the risk; it cannot remove it. guard treats text from pages, tools, and users as data, never as instructions, and holds back what looks like a hidden command. When it is unsure it says unclear instead of guessing. No detector can promise complete protection, and JERP does not.

What do true, false, and not a fact mean?

True: a source confirms it. False: a source contradicts it. Not a fact: it is opinion or marketing — “the #1 choice”, “market leader” — and cannot be checked, so your AI should not state it as fact. Each verdict carries a confidence score.

What does JERP store about the text I send?

By default, not the text. A receipt keeps a hash of the input, labels, and the policy and model versions, not your words. Storing text happens only if you opt in. Logs hold no texts and no keys. Receipts are kept for the plan period. The 1-hour key expires on its own after an hour. Details are on the privacy page and the security page.

Is JERP secure?

Keys are sent only in the Authorization header, and a key in the URL is rejected. Only a keyed hash of an API key is stored. Sign-in for MCP hosts uses OAuth with PKCE; an access token lasts 1 hour. JERP has no security certification and has not had an external penetration test. What is live, what is policy, and how to report a problem is on the security page.

Where does JERP stand on the EU AI Act?

JERP is built to stay outside the high-risk uses. Its rules forbid individual scores of employees, ranking of people, and emotion recognition at work, and it is not for decisions about people in areas such as hiring, education admission, credit, insurance, or access to essential services. Every verdict comes from an automated system and is marked as such. This is JERP's own position, not a certification.

Is JERP a replacement for Ahrefs or Semrush?

No. Those tools crawl the web for keywords, links, and rankings. JERP checks the sites and claims your agent is already working with — real competitor or noise, fits your niche or not, sourced or not. Many teams use both.

Does JERP measure brand mentions in ChatGPT or other AI answers?

No. JERP does not track how often a brand appears in AI answers. It makes the answers your own agent gives you checkable.

Do you return Google rankings?

No. JERP orders sites by how well they answer your question, not by search position. It is not a SERP tracker.

Where does JERP get its information?

From the sites themselves and from official public records, such as company registers. Every field in an answer names its source. The free tier works on sites you name or sites already in the JERP open index; it does not crawl arbitrary URLs on demand.

Do I need an account or a credit card?

The 1-hour key needs no account. Free and Personal do. Sign in with Google or GitHub for Free: 1,000 requests a day. Personal is $0 a month for 5,000 requests and is not in public access yet.

Is Pro or Team live yet?

Not yet. Pro ($9 a month) and Team ($8 per seat a month, minimum 3 seats) are not in public access yet. Free is 1,000 requests a day. Personal is $0 a month for 5,000 requests and is not in public access yet. For a higher limit or more tools, use Contact Sales.

How do I use JERP in Claude?

Without an account, the connector URL ends in /open/mcp and uses the 1-hour key. With an account, the URL ends in /mcp and you choose Sign in. Then, in a chat, open + → Connectors and turn JERP on. Ready-made prompts are in the docs; all hosts are on the integrations page. Localhost URLs do not work.

How do I use JERP in Cursor?

For the 1-hour key, copy the mcp.json snippet from the open MCP docs into .cursor/mcp.json. Its URL ends in /open/mcp. Replace jerp_YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY with your key and restart Cursor. An account connects to the URL ending in /mcp and signs in. That setup is in the account MCP docs.

Can I use JERP inside ChatGPT?

On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, and Edu, yes: add a custom MCP app on the web (developer mode → Apps → Create, URL ending in /mcp, Sign in with your key). ChatGPT Free cannot attach a custom MCP server, and Pro developer mode is read-only, which is not enough. If you run your own agent, call the REST API instead.

How do I call JERP from my own agent?

Call the REST API with Authorization: Bearer and your key, or attach the streamable HTTP MCP endpoint at /mcp. Both expose the same three tools (REST and client libraries). The full contract for agents is at /llms-full.txt and /docs.md.

Why only 24 sites per call?

The cap keeps each round trip fast and cheap enough for a live chat. If you need more, your agent simply makes another call.
